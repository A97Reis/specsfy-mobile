# Especialistas mobile para planejamento de tarefas

Proponha especialistas capazes de tornar `PREP`/`EXECUTE`/`VERIFY`/`VISUAL`/`EVIDENCE` concretos:

- **implementação nativa/multiplataforma**: `specsfy-specialist-android`,
  `specsfy-specialist-ios`, `specsfy-specialist-flutter`,
  `specsfy-specialist-react-native`, `specsfy-specialist-ionic`,
  `specsfy-specialist-kmp` ou `specsfy-specialist-cmp`;
- **banco local e migrations (`[MIGRATION]`)**: `specsfy-specialist-mobile-persistence`
  e `specsfy-specialist-supabase`;
- **release e versionamento**: `specsfy-specialist-mobile-deploy`,
  `specsfy-specialist-versioning` e `specsfy-specialist-gitflow`;
- **interface e acessibilidade**: `specsfy-specialist-interface-experience`,
  `specsfy-specialist-design-system`, `specsfy-specialist-ui-design`,
  `specsfy-specialist-ux-design` e `specsfy-specialist-mobile-accessibility`.

A skill especialista não cria `tasks.md` nem altera dependências entre tarefas.
